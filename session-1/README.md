## 📘 Session Overview  

This session marks the first step in the Quantum AI Lab Tutorials 2025 series. It is designed as a structured introduction to the foundations of quantum computing. The content starts from zero knowledge and gradually introduces essential mathematical and conceptual tools needed for deeper exploration.

---

## 🧠 What You Will Learn  

This session answers critical questions and builds foundational skills:

### 🔹 Why Quantum Computing  
Understand what makes quantum computing unique and its importance in modern computational challenges.

### 🔹 Classical vs, Quantum Computing  
Learn the key differences between classical bits and quantum bits.

### 🔹 Essential Mathematical Tools  
The session covers basic linear algebra needed for quantum computing, including  
- Complex numbers  
- Vectors  
- Matrices  
- Inner products
