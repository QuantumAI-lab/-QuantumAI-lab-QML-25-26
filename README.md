## 🔭 About the Track  

This track is designed as a comprehensive introduction to quantum computing. It builds the foundational concepts and skills required to understand quantum machine learning.

The journey begins with essential mathematical tools and proceeds through quantum phenomena, algorithms, and protocols. By the end, participants will have a solid grounding in quantum theory and be ready to continue with QML-specific sessions.

---

## 🧩 Curriculum Overview  
Each session is two hours and delivered live on the scheduled date. Recordings will be available on YouTube afterward.

1. **Foundations of Quantum Computing and Linear Algebra Review**  
2. **Quantum States and Measurement Basics**  
3. **Single-Qubit Gates and Multi-Qubit Systems**  
4. **Quantum Circuits and Measurement Outcomes**  
5. **Quantum Communication Protocols. Teleportation and Superdense Coding**  
6. **Quantum Cryptography Protocols. BB84 and BB92**  
7. **Early Quantum Algorithms. Deutsch, Bernstein.Vazirani, and Simon**  
8. **Grover’s Algorithm. Quantum Search and Amplitude Amplification**  
9. **Quantum Fourier Transform (QFT)**  
10. **Quantum Phase Estimation (QPE)**  
11. **Shor’s Algorithm. Factoring with Quantum Period Finding**  
12. **Quantum Error Correction and Future Directions**

## 🌐 About EgQCC  

The Egypt Quantum Computing Community (EgQCC) is a growing network that supports learners and professionals interested in quantum technologies.  
We offer  
- Learning resources  
- Technical workshops  
- Community discussions  
- Research collaboration  
- Industry connections  

Our goal is to empower and connect quantum pioneers in Egypt and beyond.
