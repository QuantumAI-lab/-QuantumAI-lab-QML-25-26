## 📘 Session Overview  

This session introduces the fundamental concepts of multi-qubit quantum circuits. We explore how to represent multiple qubits mathematically using tensor products and investigate the behavior of two-qubit gates. Key topics include the creation of entangled states, specifically Bell states, and the fundamental implications of the No-Cloning Theorem.

---

## 🧠 What You Will Learn  

This session moves beyond single qubits to explore interactions and multi-qubit systems.

### 🔹 Two-Qubit States & Tensor Products
Understanding how to represent and manipulate the state of multiple qubits using tensor products.

### 🔹 Two-Qubit Gates
Detailed study of the CNOT (Controlled-NOT), CZ (Controlled-Z), and SWAP gates, including their matrix representations and circuit symbols.

### 🔹 Entanglement & Bell States
Generating entangled states and understanding the properties of the four Bell states.

### 🔹 Quantum Circuit Analysis
Analyzing circuits involving both single-qubit (Hadamard) and two-qubit gates to compute output states and measurement probabilities.

### 🔹 No-Cloning Theorem
Understanding the proof and significance of the impossibility of copying arbitrary quantum states.
