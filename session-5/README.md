## 📘 Session Overview  

This session explores revolutionary quantum communication protocols that leverage quantum entanglement for information transmission. We investigate quantum teleportation, which enables the transfer of quantum states without physical movement of particles, and superdense coding, which allows transmission of two classical bits using only one quantum bit. These protocols demonstrate the fundamental advantages of quantum communication over classical methods.

---

## 🧠 What You Will Learn  

This session focuses on practical quantum communication protocols that showcase the power of entanglement.

### 🔹 Quantum Teleportation
Understanding how to transmit unknown quantum states using entangled pairs and classical communication, including the step-by-step protocol and circuit implementation.

### 🔹 Superdense Coding
Learning how to encode two classical bits into a single qubit using pre-shared entanglement, effectively doubling classical channel capacity.

### 🔹 Entanglement as a Resource
Exploring how quantum entanglement serves as a valuable resource for communication protocols that exceed classical capabilities.

### 🔹 Classical Communication Requirements
Understanding the role of classical communication channels in quantum protocols and the no-signaling principle.

### 🔹 Protocol Implementation
Hands-on implementation of both teleportation and superdense coding circuits using quantum computing frameworks.
